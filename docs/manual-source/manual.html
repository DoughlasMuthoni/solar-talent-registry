1

Overview

The Solar Talent Registry is Waterlift Solar's online recruitment system for solar PV technicians. Candidates apply from their phones through links in social-media adverts; admins verify credentials, review a preliminary score, run assessments and build a pool of approved technicians.

Two kinds of technician

EPRA-licensed solar technicianHolds an EPRA solar PV technician licence, class T1, T2 or T3. The licence must be valid and is checked by an admin against EPRA's public register.
Qualified solar technicianHas a solar or electrical qualification — NITA trade test, artisan, certificate, diploma, Higher National Diploma or degree — but no EPRA licence yet. Qualifications are confirmed with the college or examining body.

Both tracks use the same application and the same scoring for experience, technical evidence and referees, so candidates can be compared side by side. Only the entry check (the credential gate) differs. EPRA-licensed candidates can also list qualifications.

People decide, not the scoreThe score only sorts applications so the strongest are seen first. It uses the length of written answers as a rough proxy for evidence, so it can be wrong. An admin must review every application, and every adverse decision (rejecting a candidate) needs a person's judgement and a written reason.

Signing in and account security

Admin accounts can see candidates' personal data, so every sign-in needs a password and a 6-digit code from an authenticator app on your phone.

Your first sign-in

  1. Open the invitation email from Waterlift Solar Careers and follow the link. Choose a password of at least 12 characters (a passphrase of several words works well).
  2. Install a free authenticator app on your phone, such as Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator.
  3. The system opens the Two-factor authentication page and shows a QR code. In the app, add an account and scan the code (or type the key shown under it).
  4. Type the 6-digit code the app shows and choose Turn on two-factor. You are taken to the dashboard.

Two-factor is mandatory: until it is set up, the admin pages send you back to the set-up page.

Signing in after that

  1. Go to careers.waterlift.co.ke/login and enter your email and password.
  2. Open your authenticator app and enter the current 6-digit code for Waterlift. Codes change every 30 seconds.

To sign out, use the arrow button beside your name at the bottom of the menu. Always sign out on shared computers.

Forgotten password

On the sign-in page, type your email and choose Forgot password?. A reset link arrives by email (check spam). The same message is shown whether or not the address belongs to an admin, so the page can't be used to discover accounts.

Lost or replaced phone

If you lose access to your authenticator appYou cannot sign in until your authenticator is reset. The system administrator (the person with access to the Supabase project) opens Authentication → Users → your account, deletes the MFA factor, and you set up two-factor again on your next sign-in. Keep at least two admins so one can always ask for help.

Adding or removing admins

New admins are invited by the system administrator (there is no public sign-up). The new admin receives an email invitation and follows the steps above. To remove an admin, ask the system administrator to deactivate the account; their past actions stay in the audit history.

Every advert should point to the application page with a short tag saying where it was posted. The dashboard then shows which channels bring in applicants.

Building a link

Start with https://careers.waterlift.co.ke/apply and add tags after a question mark, joined with &:

src?src=facebookRecords the channel. Accepted: facebook (or fb), instagram, linkedin, x, tiktok, whatsapp (or wa), youtube. Anything else counts as "Other link".

What candidates see

Knowing the candidate's side helps you answer their questions. The application is designed for phones, takes about 10 minutes, and saves answers on the candidate's device as they go.

The five steps

#StepWhat is asked
1Identity & locationFull name, mobile (Kenyan number), email, town, county, where they can work (county / region / anywhere in Kenya) and preferred type of work.
2Licence & qualificationsThe track. EPRA track: licence class T1–T3, licence number, expiry date, optional licence note and copy. Qualified track: at least one qualification (level, course, institution, year, optional certificate number). Up to 3 qualifications on either track. Consent to verification.
3Installation experienceYears of solar experience, largest PV array (kWp), optional largest inverter and battery, types of installation, and their largest project and exact role.
4Technical capabilityInverter brands/models, battery brands/chemistries, optional logger/site-audit experience, certifications and safety training, high-voltage experience, energy-auditor licence, optional certificate uploads.
5Referees & consentTwo referees (a third is optional), consent to processing, optional consent to the 24-month talent pool.

Good to know

  • Saved as they go. If the candidate closes the page, their answers are restored on the same phone. Consent boxes are never restored — they must be ticked again.
  • Uploads are optional. PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 5 MB each and 6 files per application. Every file is checked when the application is submitted; disguised or unsupported files are rejected.
  • Errors are shown on the field and the candidate cannot continue until the step is complete.
  • Repeat applicants (same email) can apply again; their stored contact details are not overwritten from the public form. Any differences are recorded for an admin to review.

Reference numbers

Each application gets a reference made of a county code, the licence class (or Q for the qualified track) and a number — for example NAK-T2-0012 or KIS-Q-0034. Ask candidates for it whenever they contact you.

Confirmation email and status page

After submitting, the candidate receives an email with their reference and a private status link valid for 30 days. The status page shows plain-language progress (received → licence/qualification check & assessment → reference checks → approved pool) and whether their licence or qualifications have been verified. It never shows the score.

From the status page the candidate can withdraw their application or request deletion of their data (see chapter 11). If the link has expired, they enter their email at careers.waterlift.co.ke/status and a new link is sent to the email on file.

Candidates list

Every application in one table. Click a candidate's name to open their page.

Finding candidates

  • Search by name, reference or town.
  • Filter by region, track, licence class, screening result and advert source. Choose Clear filters to reset.
  • Sort by score or largest PV system by clicking the column heading. The list starts sorted by score, highest first.

Reading a row

The Licence / qualification column shows the EPRA class with its verification result, or — for qualified candidates — the highest qualification and whether it has been verified. The Screening column shows the preliminary result (see Reference, A.1).

Exporting to CSV

Use Export CSV to download all applications for a spreadsheet: reference, name, location, track, licence details, highest qualification, score, screening result, stage, source and date.

Exports contain personal dataEvery export is recorded in the audit log. Store downloaded files securely, share them only with people who need them, and delete them when no longer needed.

Reviewing a candidate

The candidate page holds everything about one application, and is where most of the work happens.

Preliminary score

The score (out of 100) is made of Experience (30), Technical capability (40) and Verifiability & mobility (30). Each line shows the points earned and the maximum. If the credential gate failed, the reasons are listed (for example "Licence expired 12 days ago").

Re-score recalculates using the current scoring rules — useful after new rules are published. Earlier scores are kept in the history.

Verifying an EPRA licence

  1. Look the licence up on EPRA's public register of solar PV technicians.
  2. Compare the class, status and expiry with what the candidate entered.
  3. Choose the Register check result: Verified, Not on register, Mismatch or Expired.
  4. Write what you saw (class, status, expiry, date checked) and choose Save verification. A note is required for every result except Pending.

The expiry badge turns amber within 90 days and red once expired.

Checking qualifications

There is no public register for qualifications, so confirm each one with the issuing college or examining body (KNEC, NITA, TVET CDACC), or by seeing the original certificate.

  1. Choose the Check result: Verified, Could not confirm, or Details don't match.
  2. Write how you checked it, then choose Save check.

For qualified-track candidates, confirm at least one qualification at or above the minimum level. EPRA-licensed candidates' qualifications are optional extras.

Documents

Uploaded files (licence copies, qualification and training certificates) are listed in the Documents card. Clicking one opens it through a private link that expires after 60 seconds. Every view is recorded.

Moving through the pipeline

Choose the new stage and Update stage. Stages: New application → Technical assessment → Practical assessment → Reference checks → Approved pool, plus Rejected after review and Withdrawn.

Rejected and Withdrawn require a reason of at least 10 characters — the candidate may ask why. Scheduling an assessment moves the stage forward automatically.

Notes and history

Add notes for other admins — phone calls, documents received, referee feedback. The history below lists notes, stage changes, verifications, scores, emails and document views with who did them and when. History cannot be edited or deleted.

The page also has Contact details (tap to call or email), the candidate's Consents, an Assessments card (chapter 8), a Message composer (chapter 9) and Data protection (chapter 11).

8

Assessments

Shortlisted candidates take a technical (knowledge) and/or practical (hands-on) assessment. Results stay with the application.

Scheduling an assessment

  1. On the candidate page, choose Schedule assessment.
  2. Pick Technical or Practical, the date and time (Nairobi time), the location and any instructions.
  3. Leave Email the invitation ticked to send the candidate the details, then choose Schedule.

The application moves to the matching stage if it is not already further along.

Recording the result

After the assessment time, the candidate page and the Assessments page show it as awaiting result. Record whether it was completed (with Pass or Fail and an optional score out of 100), a no-show or cancelled, plus assessor notes.

Messages and emails

All email is sent from info@waterlift.co.ke as "Waterlift Solar Careers". Replies go to that inbox.

Messaging one candidate

Use the Message card on the candidate page. Pick a template or write your own, then Send email. The message appears in the candidate's history.

Messaging a group

  1. Choose who: filter by stage, region, track, licence class and screening result. The count updates as you go. Rejected and withdrawn candidates are excluded unless you pick that stage.
  2. Pick a template — Request documents, Ask for clarification, Licence renewal, Welcome to the pool, Not progressing — or write your own.
  3. Use {{name}} for each candidate's first name and {{reference}} for their reference.
  4. Choose Review & send, check the summary, and confirm.
Sending limitsA group send is limited to 100 candidates. The email service's free plan allows 300 emails a day across the whole system, including automatic emails — narrow the filters if needed.

Email log

The bottom of the Messages page lists every email the system has sent, with type, recipient, subject, who sent it and status: Sent Not sent (email not configured) or Failed.

Scoring rules

The preliminary score sorts applications so the strongest are reviewed first. Its weights and thresholds are set on the Scoring rules page and are versioned, so you can always see which rules produced a score.

How a result is decided

  1. Credential gate. EPRA track: a licence class T1–T3, a licence number, an expiry date that has not passed, and consent to verification. Qualified track: at least one qualification at or above the minimum level (default: artisan certificate / NITA trade test) and consent. If the gate fails the result is Ineligible (credentials), whatever the score.
  2. Score out of 100 from experience, technical capability and verifiability & mobility.
  3. Routing by threshold (defaults): Priority review 80+, Standard review 65–79, Needs clarification 50–64, Below threshold under 50.
Ineligible and below-threshold are not rejectionsAlways confirm with the candidate before acting — for example, they may have renewed their licence or mistyped the expiry date.

Changing the rules

  1. Edit the numbers. Text-answer rules have three values: the detail needed (characters), points if detailed, and points otherwise. The banner shows the maximum per section — aim for a total of exactly 100.
  2. Set Qualified track: minimum qualification if you want a stricter gate (for example Diploma or higher).
  3. Choose Preview impact to see how open applications would move between results — nothing is saved.
  4. Describe what changed and why, decide whether to re-score all open applications now, and choose Publish as v…. A new version is created; earlier scores keep the version they were made with.

The editor will not publish invalid rules, for example thresholds out of order or weights that make Priority review impossible to reach. The Version history shows who published each version and why.

Data protection

The system is designed around Kenya's Data Protection Act 2019. These features help Waterlift meet its obligations — they don't replace good judgement.

Consent

Candidates give consent to recruitment processing and to verification of their licence and qualifications; joining the talent pool is optional. Each consent is stored with the date and the version of the privacy notice they saw. The Consents card on the candidate page shows them.

How long data is kept

SituationWhat happens
Application open or in the approved poolKept.
Rejected or withdrawnErased automatically 12 months after the application closed.
Rejected or withdrawn, with talent-pool consentErased automatically 24 months after closing.

The check runs every day. Erasure removes the candidate, their applications and their uploaded files, leaving only a record that an erasure happened (with no personal details).

Requests from candidates

  • Withdrawal — candidates can withdraw from their status page; the stage changes to Withdrawn and appears in the history as done by the candidate.
  • Deletion — a request from the status page shows a red Data deletion requested badge on the candidate page and in the history. Act on it promptly.
  • Access or correction — handle by email from info@waterlift.co.ke; the CSV export and candidate page show what is held.

Erasing a candidate

  1. On the candidate page, choose Erase candidate data.
  2. Type the application reference exactly to confirm.
  3. Confirm. All their applications, documents and contact details are permanently deleted.
This cannot be undoneCheck you have the right candidate first.

Audit trail and security

  • Every verification, stage change, note, score, export, email and document view is recorded with who did it and when.
  • Documents are stored privately and opened only through short-lived links.
  • Candidates never see scores, screening results or admin notes.
  • Use your own account only, keep two-factor on, and never forward exports or documents to personal email.
Privacy noticeThe public notice is at careers.waterlift.co.ke/privacy. It is a draft awaiting review by Waterlift's data protection lead; any change to it or to the consent wording must be versioned by the system administrator.

Troubleshooting and FAQ

QuestionAnswer
I get "That code didn't match".Use the current code — they change every 30 seconds. Check your phone's time is set automatically; a wrong clock produces wrong codes.
I'm signed in but sent to the two-factor page.Two-factor isn't set up on your account yet. Complete the set-up (chapter 2).
"This account does not have admin access."Your login exists but isn't an active admin. Ask the system administrator.
A candidate didn't get the confirmation email.Ask them to check spam. Look in the email log (Messages page) for its status. They can request a new status link at /status.
A candidate says they can't continue on the form.Each step shows a red message under the field that needs attention. Common causes: phone not a Kenyan number, licence expiry missing, no qualification added on the qualified track, or a consent box not ticked.
A candidate chose the wrong track.Ask them to apply again choosing the right track (the same email is fine), then withdraw the first application with a reason.
A licensed candidate shows "Ineligible (credentials)".Open the score card: the reasons are listed. Usually an expired or missing expiry date. Confirm with the candidate and ask for renewal evidence.
A group email says too many recipients.Group sends are limited to 100. Add filters (region, stage, track) and send in batches.
Scores changed overnight.Someone published new scoring rules and re-scored. Check Scoring rules → Version history.
Where do candidates' replies go?To the info@waterlift.co.ke inbox. Record anything important as a note on the candidate.
